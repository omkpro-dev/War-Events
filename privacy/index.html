Privacy

War Events is a free map of the world's conflicts. We don't run ads, we don't use cookies, and we don't track you.

  • No cookies, no advertising, no tracking or analytics profiles.
  • We don't ask for your name or account details to use the site.
  • We never sell or share your information.

What we collect

Nothing, if you just browse. You can use the map, stories, feed and country pages without giving us any personal information.

A daily page-view count. We may count how many times the site is opened each day. It's a single number that isn't linked to you, and it resets every day.

Push notifications in the iPhone app. If you allow notifications in the War Events app, Apple and Google's Firebase service give the app an anonymous device token so we can send alerts about major stories. You can turn this off at any time in your phone's Settings.

Stored on your device

The site saves a few small settings in your browser so it remembers your choices, such as which panels you had open, the map layer you picked, and whether you've seen the welcome message. These stay on your device, are never sent to us, and you can clear them at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Services the site loads from

To show the map and news, your browser loads some files directly from other providers. Like any website, they can see standard connection details such as your IP address and browser type. We don't control how they handle that information:

  • Vercel hosts the site.
  • Google Fonts provides the typefaces.
  • jsDelivr provides the country border data.
  • GitHub hosts the regularly updated story and war data.
  • NASA provides satellite imagery of the Earth.
  • flagcdn.com provides country flags.
  • rss2json is used to read some public news feeds.

Links to news sources and Wikipedia take you to those sites, which have their own privacy policies.

How stories are written

Story summaries, the Feed and the WIRE ticker, key points and impact scores are generated automatically from public news reporting using an AI model (Claude, by Anthropic), in original wording. We do not show publishers' photos. Only the public article is sent for this. No information about visitors is ever included.

Children

War Events covers armed conflict and is meant for a general adult audience. We don't knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
