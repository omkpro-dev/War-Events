Copyright and takedown

War Events is a free, non-commercial map and news reader. We respect publishers' and creators' rights, and if something here concerns your work you can reach a person directly.

  • Email admin@entropyadvisory.com to ask for something to be corrected or removed.
  • We acknowledge every request within two working days and act on valid ones promptly.
  • We don't use outlets' photos or videos, and we don't republish their headlines or articles.

What this site does with news

Links, not copies. Every story points back to the original article and names the outlet that published it. We don't host or reproduce articles.

Summaries in our own words. The story summaries, the Feed and the WIRE ticker are written automatically in original wording using an AI model (Claude, by Anthropic). They report facts, not an outlet's phrasing, and are checked against copying a source's wording.

Coverage & Bias. This view lists which outlets covered a story, with a link to each, so readers can compare coverage themselves.

No outlet imagery. We don't show publishers' photos or videos. The map, country outlines, flags and emblems are our own or openly licensed.

Asking for a removal or correction

Send an email to admin@entropyadvisory.com with:

  • Your name and, if you act for an organisation, which one.
  • The address of the page on War Events, or a description of what you're referring to.
  • The original work it concerns (a link to it) and what you'd like us to do: remove it, change it, or stop linking to a specific article.
  • A line confirming you are the rights holder or authorised to act for them.

We'll reply to confirm we've received it, and we'll take the item down or fix it while we look into it where the request looks valid. If we need more detail we'll ask.

Factual errors

Summaries are generated automatically and can be wrong. If you spot a mistake, use the same address and tell us which story it is. We'd rather fix it than leave it up.

Other rights

Nothing on this page limits any rights you have under the law where you live. Where a notice is sent to us under a specific law, such as a copyright notice, we'll deal with it as that law requires.
